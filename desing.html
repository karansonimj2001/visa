Frequently Asked Regulatory Questions

Can I convert this 30-day visa into a UAE residency or employment visa? expand_more

Yes. Under current UAE immigration policy, you can transition your tourist status to an employment or golden residency visa via an internal status change without exiting the country, provided the prospective sponsor completes the administrative filing before your 30-day term lapses.

What is the financial penalty for overstaying beyond the 30-day limit? expand_more

The UAE government applies a standardized fine of AED 50 per day of unauthorized stay following the expiration of your visa grace window, plus administrative exit clearance fees at the border. You are advised to request a 30-day extension prior to expiration.
